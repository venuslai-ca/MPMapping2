1 · Brief

Objective

Turn FSA-level ad performance into map-based guidance for regional media allocation: where response is strong or weak against a chosen benchmark, and what to do about it.

Context

  • Client and agency as named in the original FSA-Based Ad Performance Database concept (Aug 12, 2026).
  • Farmer audiences are geo-bounded: local crop, weather, soil and retailer conditions make location a stronger signal than demographics.

Data in hand

  • 3 tabs, 279 FSA-campaign rows, 141 FSAs, 10.4M impressions, 193.8K clicks.
  • Battle of the Bushels (Seed), Win-Win and No Flex TruFlex (Traits); Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba plus 3 BC FSAs.
  • Fields: FSA, Province, Portfolio, Campaign, Impressions, Clicks, CTR.
  • Not yet available: date, channel, creative, spend, revenue.
  • Data quality: 3 BC FSAs (V1G, V0C, V1J) sit outside the Prairies; 36 rows have under 5,000 impressions; 21 zero-delivery rows are set to no CTR.

Phases

  • 1 · Now: cleaned dataset and interactive map on impressions, clicks, CTR.
  • 2 · Next: Q&A assistant grounded in dashboard data only.
  • 3 · Later: spend, channel, trend and sales/CRM linkage.

Benchmark logic

  • Planner picks national or province average, calculated per campaign so Seed and Traits are never blended.
  • Index = actual clicks ÷ clicks expected at the benchmark CTR.
  • Low-volume FSAs (default under 5,000 impressions) are flagged, and judgement is held.

Guardrails

  • CTR measures engagement, not sales; no causal claims.
  • Respect platform privacy thresholds; aggregated, non-personal data only.
  • FSA points are representative locations; rural FSAs cover large areas.
